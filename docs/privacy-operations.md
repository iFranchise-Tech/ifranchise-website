# Privacy operations handover — 25 October 2026

The website changes support privacy handling; they are not legal certification of DPDP compliance.

Implemented: distinct Privacy Policy and Terms; Privacy choices page; purpose-specific, unchecked consent and notice-version records; age confirmation for enquiries/applications; no optional analytics attribution retained while inactive; minimal privacy-request intake; super-admin-only privacy records; metadata-only admin access logs; status workflow with verification/completion confirmations. Privacy requests are excluded from the lead workbook. Existing lead/application tabs remain in the one authorised workbook.

Before confirming organisational compliance, the business must:

1. Assign responsibility for the published Privacy & Grievance Contact inbox (contact@ifranchise.in), response coverage and escalation. The public notice omits personal names/designations at the owner's request; obtain legal advice about any applicable named-officer obligations, including the existing SPDI framework.
2. Approve a retention schedule covering enquiry/application records, consent evidence, resumes, logs, workbook copies, email and backups, with legal holds and applicable minimum periods. Do not enable blanket deletion without this review.
3. Verify least-privilege access and processor arrangements for hosting, Google Sheets/Apps Script, email and any downstream recipients. Code review cannot establish those account settings or contractual safeguards.
4. Verify a requester's identity proportionately before disclosure or alteration. Stop follow-up across all relevant systems for withdrawal. Review erasure requests against retention/legal holds; act on website, workbook, resume and communication copies. The admin status control records progress and does not itself erase data or send a reply.
5. Record the outcome, communicate it, and only then mark a request Completed. The 30-day admin review target is an internal operational target, not a claim of a statutory deadline.
6. Establish incident response, breach assessment/notification, restoration and access-review procedures. Test the public request path and monitoring of pending requests periodically.
